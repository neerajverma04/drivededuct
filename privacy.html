DriveDeduct: Mileage & Taxes

Official Privacy Policy & Data Disclosure

Effective Date: September 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to DriveDeduct: Mileage & Taxes, developed by Neeraj Verma ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Android and iOS applications.

DriveDeduct is designed for gig workers, delivery drivers, rideshare drivers, and self-employed individuals who need to track business mileage for tax deductions (IRS, CRA, HMRC, ATO, etc.).

Core Principle: DriveDeduct is built with a 100% privacy-first approach. All core tracking and receipt scanning features work completely on-device. Your trip routes, dollar amounts, and receipt photos never pass through any central tracking server. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly connect your personal Google Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive.

2. Information We Collect

2a. Data You Provide

  • Trip notes, vehicle information, and mileage logs you manually enter
  • Receipt images you scan within the app for fuel, maintenance, or business expenses
  • Cloud account credentials (Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive) for optional backup & sync — only if you explicitly authorize them via OAuth 2.0

2b. Data Collected Automatically

  • GPS coordinates while the tracking foreground service is active (for trip route calculation and deadhead mile detection)
  • Start and stop timestamps of trips
  • Distance calculations and speed telemetry derived solely from on-device GPS data

2c. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT collect user identity, contacts, or behavioral advertising identifiers
  • We do NOT track you across third-party apps or websites
  • We do NOT sell, lease, or monetize your data to data brokers or advertisers — ever
  • We do NOT have access to files in your cloud storage accounts beyond the isolated DriveDeduct folder you authorize

3. Device Permissions & Why We Use Them

📍 Location (Fine, Coarse & Background Location)

Required for automatic GPS drive tracking. Fine location captures precise route data needed for audit-proof logbooks. Background location is used so tracking continues smoothly while the app is minimized, in standby, or running your GPS navigation app (Google Maps, Waze). You can stop tracking at any time directly from the persistent notification or within the app.

📷 Camera & Photo Library Access

Used exclusively for the smart receipt scanner powered by Google ML Kit OCR. All text recognition is performed 100% on-device — receipt photos are never uploaded to any remote OCR server.

🔔 Notifications

Used to display a persistent notification while GPS tracking is active (required by Android for foreground services), giving you real-time distance, speed, and 1-tap stop controls.

🔵 Bluetooth (Nearby Devices)

Optional. Used to detect connection or disconnection from your car's Bluetooth audio or OBD-II adapter to automatically start and stop trip tracking without needing to touch your phone. Bluetooth telemetry is never shared or transmitted off-device.

🔄 Boot Receiver (Auto-resume)

Allows an interrupted drive tracking session to cleanly resume after an unexpected device restart, ensuring no deductible miles are lost.

🌐 Internet & Network State

Used only when you choose to sync your encrypted backups to Google Drive, Dropbox, or Microsoft OneDrive, or fetch currency/tax rates. The entire app is fully functional offline without an active internet connection.

🔋 Battery Optimization Exemption

DriveDeduct may request exemption from aggressive OS battery killers so Android does not terminate GPS tracking in the middle of a trip. The app incorporates dynamic distance filtering and stationary sleep to minimize power draw.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To calculate accurate trip mileage and compute deductible tax savings for IRS, CRA, HMRC, and ATO returns
  • To extract total amounts, tax lines, and merchant names from receipt photos using on-device OCR
  • To calculate the difference between platform-reported miles and actual business miles (Discrepancy Auditor)
  • To sync encrypted backups to your personally linked cloud storage provider
  • To generate tamper-resistant PDF and CSV audit logs for tax authorities and platform disputes

5. Cloud Storage & Third-Party Services

DriveDeduct optionally integrates with the following cloud storage platforms for backup and restore. All sync is strictly opt-in and client-side:

  • Google Drive — governed by Google Privacy Policy
  • Dropbox — governed by Dropbox Privacy Policy
  • Microsoft OneDrive — governed by Microsoft Privacy Statement

We only access the isolated DriveDeduct application folder you authorize via official OAuth 2.0 PKCE flow. We never receive or store your passwords.

Google ML Kit Text Recognition

Receipt OCR operates entirely on your device via Google ML Kit. No receipt data or photos are transmitted to Google. See ML Kit Privacy disclosure.

Google Play Billing & RevenueCat

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are handled securely through Google Play Billing. Payment credentials and card numbers are processed exclusively by Google and never touch our code.

7. Data Storage & Security

Your mileage logs, inspection records, and receipt photos are stored in secure, private device storage. Cloud backups are encrypted using client-side AES-GCM encryption before leaving the device.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

You hold 100% ownership and control over your records. You can delete individual drives, purge receipt archives, or wipe all application data instantly from the Settings menu. Uninstalling the app permanently purges all local data.

9. Children's Privacy

DriveDeduct is intended for professional use by adults (18+) operating commercial or rideshare vehicles. We do not knowingly collect or solicit information from anyone under the age of 13.

10. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Global)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have full rights to:

  • Access & Portability: Export complete trip history and receipt attachments at any time in open CSV and PDF formats
  • Erasure: Permanently delete all logs and local databases directly within the app
  • Opt-Out: Stop automatic Bluetooth tracking, cloud backups, or location permissions at any time
