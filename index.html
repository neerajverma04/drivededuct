Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about DriveDeduct and tax tracking

Will DriveDeduct drain my phone battery?

No! DriveDeduct was built with dynamic dual-tier power management. When you stop moving at a traffic light or park, the GPS switches to stationary battery-saving sleep mode. Average battery consumption is less than 1.5% per hour.

Does tracking work while my screen is turned off?

Yes. DriveDeduct utilizes an Android foreground service with a persistent status notification to ensure the OS never kills the GPS service while you're driving.

How does it find miles that Uber or DoorDash missed?

Platforms report only "On-Trip" miles (from the moment you pick up food/passengers to drop-off). DriveDeduct records the entire continuous business shift: driving between orders, waiting in hotspots, and returning to your home zone — which are 100% legal write-offs under IRS & CRA rules.

Are DriveDeduct's PDF reports accepted by the IRS and CRA?

Yes. Our PDF reports are formatted to satisfy IRS Revenue Procedure 2010-51 and CRA Logbook rules, featuring date, origin, destination, vehicle, business purpose, and cryptographic tamper-resistant verification.

Privacy Policy – DriveDeduct: Mileage & Taxes

Effective Date: September 20, 2026 • Developed by Neeraj Verma

View Full Page Version
Core Privacy Principle: DriveDeduct operates on a 100% on-device architecture. All GPS coordinates, route histories, receipt photographs, and tax deduction figures are stored locally on your device. We run zero tracking or telemetry servers. Your data never leaves your device unless you choose to sync with your private Google Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive.

1. Data Collected & Processed

  • Precise & Background Location: Required solely to record driving distance and map routes while tracking is active. Stops immediately when tracking is ended.
  • Camera & Photos: Accessed only for receipt scanning. Text is extracted locally using Google ML Kit on-device OCR. Images are never uploaded to any remote OCR service.
  • Bluetooth: Used optionally to trigger auto-start/stop when connected to your vehicle's Bluetooth hands-free unit.

2. Zero Data Selling or Third-Party Analytics

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your location data, financial records, or personal information to third parties, data brokers, or advertisers.

3. Subscriptions & Billing

All in-app purchases and subscriptions ($4.99/mo, $49.99/yr, $99.99 lifetime) are securely managed through the official Google Play Billing system. We never receive or store your payment details.

4. Contact Information

For any privacy inquiries or data requests, please contact developer Neeraj Verma directly at neerajverma04@gmail.com.
